03 / How it works

From an idea
to a single press.

Choose what you use most.
TOXIQ gives it a key of its own.

  1. 01

    Connect.

    Plug in your TOXIQ and open the Configurator.

  2. 02

    Assign.

    Choose a text macro, shortcut or action for each key, then save it to the device.

  3. 03

    Press.

    Trigger your action in the game or app you are using. Your mappings stay on the device.
